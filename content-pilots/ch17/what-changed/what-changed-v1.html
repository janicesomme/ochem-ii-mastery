NO FEAR OCHEM · CHAPTER 17 · VISUAL V1

What changed?
Does it matter?

Two drawings can look different and still use the same reaction rule. A small change can also change the answer.

Your job

  1. Compare A with B. Name what changed.
  2. Decide whether you can use the same reaction move.
  3. Predict each product, then reveal the drawings.

There is a middle answer: sometimes the bond-changing move stays the same, but the product type changes. Pair 5 shows this.

These are original practice examples. Amber outlines in the answers mark the reacting group or the comparison group; they do not represent charge. All molecule structures are skeletal drawings. On a narrow screen, scroll the diagrams sideways.

Three checks to keep

What group is here? Look at the carbon double-bonded to oxygen and its neighbors.

What does this reagent do to that group? A new reagent name may—or may not—change the product.

Did only the picture change? A longer chain or a different orientation can leave the reaction rule unchanged.
